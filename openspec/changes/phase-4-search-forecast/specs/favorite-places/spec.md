# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Favorites saved from the forecast appear in the list
A place saved with the favorite toggle on its forecast SHALL appear in the favorites list without restarting the app, and a place removed there SHALL disappear from it, even though the two screens belong to independent features.

#### Scenario: Save then go back
- **WHEN** the user saves Tarapoto from its forecast and goes back to the favorites screen
- **THEN** Tarapoto is in the list with its temperature
